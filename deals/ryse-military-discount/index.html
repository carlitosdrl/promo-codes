RYSE Military Discount

How the military & first responder discount works at helloryse.com and who’s eligible.

Who’s eligible

  • Current & former U.S. military
  • Military spouses & dependents
  • First responders: law enforcement, fire, and EMS

Source: RYSE’s official military discount page.

How to redeem

  1. Go to the official page: helloryse.com/pages/military-discount
  2. Click the verification button and complete the process (via their verification partner).
  3. Receive a single-use code (or automatic discount) and apply it at checkout.

RYSE has publicly referenced savings for military and first responders (e.g., up to 10% in past promos). Final amount depends on the current campaign.

FAQs

Does this stack with other promo codes?

Stacking depends on current terms displayed at checkout. If stacking isn’t allowed, the system will apply only one offer.

Is verification required every time?

Typically you verify to obtain a code for that purchase. Some providers issue single-use codes per order.

Who counts as a first responder?

According to RYSE: law enforcement, fire, and EMS are included.
