Install RYSE SmartShade: step-by-step

This guide walks you through mounting, calibration, app setup, and common fixes — with photos.

Before you start

  • Check that your bead chain fits the required size.
  • Tools: screwdriver/drill, level, pencil, measuring tape.
  • Charge the device fully before mounting.

Mounting (5–10 minutes)

  1. Mark the mounting position beside your shade.
  2. Pre-drill holes if needed; attach the bracket firmly.
  3. Attach the SmartShade body to the bracket.
  4. Route the chain and ensure smooth movement.

Calibration & app setup (5–10 minutes)

  1. Open the app and add a new device.
  2. Run calibration: set fully open and fully closed positions.
  3. Create your first schedule (e.g., open at 7:00, close at sunset).

Automations & scenes

Group multiple windows, set scenes for “Movie Night”, or tie shades to routines with your preferred voice assistant (hub may be required).

Troubleshooting

  • Shade stops early: re-run calibration and verify chain tension.
  • Device feels noisy: check mount stability and chain alignment.
  • Battery drains fast: reduce schedule frequency or add a solar panel.
